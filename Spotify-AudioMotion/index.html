Privacy Statement

Why do we need Microphone access?

This visualizer runs entirely in your browser. Spotify does not provide "raw audio data" to websites due to copyright restrictions. To bypass this and create real visualizations, we listen to your system's audio output (loopback) via the microphone permission.

Data Safety:

  • No audio is ever recorded.
  • No audio is ever sent to a server.
  • The data is processed instantly for visuals and discarded immediately (Real-time FFT).
